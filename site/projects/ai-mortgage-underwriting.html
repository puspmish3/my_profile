The business challenge

An annual volume of approximately 38,000 loans made document review, verification, and conditions clearing a major operational workload. Loan cycle time averaged 42 days, and post-close defects stood at 2.4%. Faster file preparation needed to coexist with human credit decisions and model-risk controls.

The architecture behind the outcome

I architected an underwriting platform embedded in Encompass. It combines document AI, income and asset verification agents, a guideline copilot, and automated clearing of routine conditions. The platform prepares the file so that underwriters can focus on the credit decision.

Delivery & decision ownership

AI prepares the file. A human underwriter makes the credit decision.
  • Embed document intelligence and income and asset verification into Encompass.
  • Support underwriters with a guideline copilot and automate the clearing of routine conditions.
  • Retain every credit decision with a human underwriter.
  • Build fair-lending testing and SR 11-7 model-risk controls into the design from day one.
